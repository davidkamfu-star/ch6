DATIVE COVALENT BOND

O: → Cu²⁺

Each water ligand donates one lone pair from oxygen to the copper(II) centre.

WHERE DOES THE BONDING PAIR COME FROM?

Ligand donates; metal ion accepts.

The ligand is a Lewis base with a lone pair. Cu²⁺ is a Lewis acid that accepts that electron pair into an available orbital.

Key idea: both electrons in the new bond originally come from the ligand. Once formed, the dative bond behaves like an ordinary covalent bond.
